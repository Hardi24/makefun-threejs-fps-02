Dustline Yard is a round-based tactical FPS in three.js: three Vanguard defenders hold the Foundry and Depot sites against four Raider bots who plant a charge. Rounds are won by elimination, detonation or defuse; money earned each round buys five weapons, armour and Spark-9 frag grenades. Bots patrol waypoints, hold posts, call out sightings, escort the carrier and lob grenades at marks they cannot see.

## Run it

```sh
node entry/serve.ts --port 8080
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Click to play: WASD to move, mouse to look, click to fire, right click to aim, G to throw a frag grenade, E to defuse, Tab for the scoreboard.
